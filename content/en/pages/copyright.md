This page explains who owns the content on KTrend (ktrend.kr) and how you may use it.

## 1. Ownership

Copyright in the **articles, editorial selection, site design, source code and data structures** on this site belongs to the operator of KTrend.

Under Korean copyright law, which follows the principle of automatic protection, copyright arises when a work is created — no registration is required.

## 2. What is protected, and what is not

Copyright protects **expression**, not **facts**. That distinction matters a great deal on a site like this one, which deals largely in dates and schedules.

| Type | Example | Protected? |
|---|---|---|
| Factual information | "Chuseok 2026 falls on September 25" | No |
| Expression | The sentences, structure and framing used to explain it | **Yes** |
| Selection and arrangement | Which events appear in the calendar and how they are organised | **Yes** (compilation) |
| Tables and diagrams | The structure of a comparison table we built | **Yes** |
| Design and code | Layout, styling, build scripts | **Yes** |

So **stating a date you learned here is not infringement.** Copying our sentences, tables or structure is.

## 3. Note on AI-assisted content

Some articles on this site are **drafted with AI assistance and then reviewed, fact-checked and edited by the operator before publication.**

Under Korean copyright law, output generated by AI without human involvement is not, in principle, recognised as a copyrightable work. However, where a human adds creative contribution — through revision, addition, editing or arrangement — that contribution may be protected, according to guidance from Korea's Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea Copyright Commission.

Accordingly, this site's policy is:

- Every article is **fact-checked and edited by the operator** before publication.
- Topic selection, structure, and the arrangement of tables and FAQs are editorial decisions.
- Information that is not yet confirmed is explicitly marked, so readers can distinguish fact from expectation.

## 4. Quoting and republishing

### Permitted

- **Quotation with attribution** — reasonable quotation is permitted. Please name KTrend and link to the specific article.
- **Linking** — you may link to any page on this site freely.
- **Factual information** — dates, schedules and similar facts may be used freely.

Suggested attribution:

> Source: KTrend (https://ktrend.kr/en/posts/article-slug/)

### Requires prior permission

- Reproducing or republishing an article in full or in substantial part
- Translation, or producing and distributing summaries
- Creating derivative works for commercial purposes
- Automated scraping for bulk reproduction or republication
- Use as AI training data

## 5. Images and third-party material

This site does not republish other people's copyrighted material without permission.

- We do not copy news article text or photographs. Where we report facts, we verify them and write our own account.
- Where we rely on an external source, we link to it.

## 6. Reporting a copyright concern

If you believe content on this site infringes your rights, please tell us via the [contact page](/contact/). We will review it and correct or remove the material promptly. Including the following speeds things up:

- The URL of the page in question
- What the infringing content is, and where on the page
- Information establishing that you hold the rights

If you find our content republished elsewhere without permission, we would also appreciate hearing about it.

Content on this site is for general information only and is not professional financial, legal or medical advice. Dates and regulations can change — always confirm with the official announcement.
