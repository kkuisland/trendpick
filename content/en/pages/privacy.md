This policy explains what information this site collects and how it is used. It applies to the English section of KTrend at ktrend.kr.

## 1. Information we collect

You can read this site without creating an account, and we do not ask you to submit personal information. Some data is collected automatically as part of normal web operation:

- Access logs, including IP address, browser and device type, and pages visited
- Cookies set by the third-party services described below

## 2. Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertising through the following services:

- **Google AdSense.** Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). For EEA, UK and Swiss visitors, ad personalisation is subject to consent.
- **Kakao AdFit.** Cookies may be used in the course of serving advertising.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some site functions may be affected.

## 3. Affiliate links

This site participates in affiliate programmes, including Coupang Partners and travel booking affiliate programmes. If you make a purchase or booking through one of these links, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Where an article contains affiliate links, a disclosure is displayed at the top of that article. Affiliate relationships do not determine what we recommend.

## 4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which articles are read and how visitors reach the site. This data is aggregated and used to improve our content. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data in accordance with [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Data retention and sharing

Automatically collected data is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above. **We do not sell personal information to third parties.**

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to object to certain processing. Requests can be sent via our [contact page](/contact/).

## 7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## 8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page. This policy is effective as of August 21, 2026.
