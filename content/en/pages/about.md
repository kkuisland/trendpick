**KTrend** explains Korea to people outside it.

We cover what Korea is watching, eating and talking about — K-dramas, Korean food, holidays and the big events on the national calendar — written for readers who don't speak Korean and don't have a local friend to ask.

## What we publish

- **K-Drama** — what's airing, where to watch it legally in your country, and the context that makes it make sense.
- **Korean Food** — what dishes actually are, how to order them, and what to expect at the table.
- **Culture** — holidays, etiquette, and the everyday customs that guidebooks skip.
- **Travel** — practical timing and logistics for visiting Korea.
- **Trending** — the Korean news and internet moments people are searching for right now.

## How we work

1. **Accuracy first.** Dates, prices and rules come from official sources. Where something isn't confirmed yet, we say so rather than guessing.
2. **We update instead of republishing.** When a schedule is confirmed or a rule changes, we revise the original article and show the updated date.
3. **Ads and affiliate links are labelled.** Where an article contains affiliate links, a disclosure appears at the top of that article — not buried in the footer.

This site also publishes in Korean at [ktrend.kr](/). The Korean and English sections are written separately rather than machine-translated, because what a Korean reader needs to know about Chuseok and what a visitor needs to know are different things.
