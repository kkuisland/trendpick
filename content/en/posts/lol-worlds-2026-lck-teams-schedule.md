The 2026 League of Legends World Championship runs **October 15 to November 14, 2026**, across three U.S. cities — Los Angeles, Allen (Texas), and Brooklyn, New York. The Play-In Stage opened on October 15 in Los Angeles, the Swiss Stage starts October 23 in Allen, and the final is **November 14 at Barclays Center (2 PM EST)**. All four Korean teams — **Gen.G, Hanwha Life Esports, T1 and Dplus KIA** — skip the Play-In and start in the Swiss Stage. T1 arrives as the three-time defending champion (2023–2025) with six World titles overall, while Gen.G won the 2026 LCK title and Hanwha Life Esports won MSI 2026.

## Full schedule: play-in to final

Nineteen teams take part. The tournament moves from Los Angeles to Allen, Texas, and finishes at Brooklyn's Barclays Center. Start times below are the first match of each day, as listed in LoL Esports' official Worlds 2026 primer.

| Stage | Local dates | First match (local) | First match (Korea, KST) | Venue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Play-In Stage | Oct 15–18 | 11 AM PDT | 3 AM, next day | Riot Games Arena, Los Angeles |
| Swiss Stage, week 1 | Oct 23–26 | 12 PM CDT | 2 AM, next day | Credit Union of Texas Event Center, Allen, TX |
| Swiss Stage, week 2 | Oct 28–30 | 3 PM CDT | 5 AM, next day | Credit Union of Texas Event Center, Allen, TX |
| Swiss Stage, final day | Oct 31 | 12 PM CDT | 2 AM, next day | Credit Union of Texas Event Center, Allen, TX |

| Final | Nov 14 | 2 PM EST | 4 AM, Nov 15 | Barclays Center, Brooklyn, NY |

U.S. daylight saving time ends on November 1, which is why the knockout start times switch from CDT to CST.

## How each stage works

- **Play-In Stage:** the third seeds from the LEC, LCS and LCP plus the CBLOL's second seed — Karmine Corp, Cloud9, MVK Esports and FURIA — play a double-elimination, best-of-five bracket. Only the winner advances to the Swiss Stage.
- **Swiss Stage:** 16 teams play up to five rounds. Three match wins sends a team to the knockouts; three losses eliminates it. After Round 1, teams with the same record are drawn against each other, and no team faces the same opponent twice.
- **Knockout Stage:** quarterfinals, semifinals and the final are all best-of-five.

Draws happen as the event goes on: Swiss Round 1 on October 18, Rounds 2–5 on October 23, 24, 26 and 30, and the quarterfinal draw on October 31. In the quarterfinal draw, the two 3-0 teams are paired against two 3-2 teams.

## LCK's four teams and how they qualified

Korea sends four teams, listed here in the Swiss Round 1 draw tiers from the official primer:

- **Gen.G (Tier 1)** — won the 2026 LCK title, beating Hanwha Life Esports 3-1 in the September 13 final.
- **Hanwha Life Esports (Tier 2)** — qualified as MSI 2026 champion after beating Bilibili Gaming 3-2 in the July 12 final in Daejeon, Korea; also the 2026 LCK runner-up.
- **T1 (Tier 3)** — finished third in the 2026 LCK playoffs.
- **Dplus KIA (Tier 4)** — took the LCK's last berth by beating KT Rolster 3-1 in the lower bracket on September 4.

In Swiss Round 1, Tier 1 teams face Tier 4 teams and Tier 2 teams face Tier 3 teams, and teams from the same region cannot meet. That means no LCK-vs-LCK match is possible until Round 2.

## Why every Korean team skips the Play-In Stage

Under Riot's official Worlds 2026 rules, the LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS and LCP each get three slots and the CBLOL gets two. Two more slots go to the MSI champion and to the region of the MSI runner-up. Hanwha Life Esports (LCK) won MSI 2026 and Bilibili Gaming (LPL) finished second, so both the LCK and the LPL have four teams. Because the Play-In is made up only of the LEC, LCS and LCP third seeds and the CBLOL's second seed, all four LCK teams — and all four LPL teams — start directly in the Swiss Stage.

## T1's record coming in

T1 is the most successful team in Worlds history, with six championships (2013, 2015, 2016, 2023, 2024 and 2025). It completed a three-peat by beating KT Rolster 3-2 in the 2025 final. This year T1 plays with Doran, Oner, Faker, Peyz and Keria, with Peyz new at the bot lane position. KT Rolster, last year's runner-up, did not qualify for Worlds 2026.

## Tickets

Riot announced that tickets for Worlds 2026 would be released in mid-to-late July. For prices, remaining seats and any further releases, rely on Riot's official LoL Esports channels rather than third-party listings, and be careful with resale offers.

## How to watch for free

You don't need a ticket or a subscription to follow the tournament. Matches stream free on the official **lolesports.com** site and on Riot Games' official Twitch channel (twitch.tv/riotgames). Open the schedule on lolesports.com to see each match's start time in your own time zone.

The final week also has a Worlds Fan Fest at Pier 36 in Lower Manhattan, November 12–14.
