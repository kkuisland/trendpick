Apple has officially confirmed a "Surprise and Shine" keynote for **September 9, 2026, at 10 a.m. Pacific Time**, streaming live from the Steve Jobs Theater at Apple Park. That date and time are the only things Apple itself has confirmed — everything about the product lineup below comes from pre-event reporting by outlets like MacRumors, 9to5Mac, and TechRadar, not an Apple announcement. Multiple reports agree Apple is splitting its iPhone launch this year, leading with the iPhone 18 Pro line and a first-ever foldable iPhone while the standard iPhone 18 waits until spring 2027.

## When is the Apple event in your timezone

The keynote starts at the same moment everywhere — only the local clock time changes. Because the U.S. is still on daylight time in September, the gap to Korea is 16 hours.

| Region | Local time | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Cupertino (PT) | 10:00 AM | Sept 9 |
| New York (ET) | 1:00 PM | Sept 9 |
| London (BST) | 6:00 PM | Sept 9 |
| Seoul / Tokyo (KST/JST) | 2:00 AM | Sept 10 |
| Sydney (AEST) | 3:00 AM | Sept 10 |

If you're in Korea and don't want to stay up, Apple always re-uploads the full keynote video within a few hours, so watching a same-day replay costs you nothing but the surprise.

## How to watch the livestream

Apple streams every keynote for free, with no sign-up needed. You can watch through any of these:

- Apple's official [Apple Events page](https://www.apple.com/apple-events/) on desktop or mobile
- The Apple TV app, on Apple devices, smart TVs, and game consoles
- Apple's official YouTube channel, which mirrors the stream in real time

## What's not coming this time

If reports are accurate, Apple is holding back its usual full lineup. The standard **iPhone 18**, a lower-cost **iPhone 18e**, and an **iPhone Air 2** are all tipped for a separate launch in **spring 2027** instead of sharing the September stage. That would be a genuine break from Apple's usual "everything at once" September event, so it's worth watching whether that split actually happens as reported.

## When will it launch in South Korea

Korea's release timing has followed a fairly consistent pattern the last three years, which is useful for guessing this year's date even though nothing is official yet.

| Model | Global announcement | Korea release | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone 15 | Sept 12, 2023 | Oct 13, 2023 | Korea in the second wave |
| iPhone 16 | Sept 9, 2024 | Sept 20, 2024 | First year Korea got a first-wave launch |
| iPhone 17 | Sept 9, 2025 | Sept 19, 2025 | Pre-order the Friday after announcement, release the following Friday |

If Apple repeats that pattern this year, pre-orders would open around **September 11** and Korea's release would land around **September 18** — but Apple has not confirmed either date, so hold off on locking in plans until the keynote. [confirm]
