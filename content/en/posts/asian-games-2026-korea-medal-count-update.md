Team Korea sits third in the medal table at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games, with 10 gold, 12 silver and 28 bronze medals — 50 total — as of September 24, behind China and host nation Japan. Shooter Choo Ka-eun and fencer Oh Sang-uk each won gold on September 24, and the men's U-23 football team plays Vietnam in the quarterfinals on September 25, chasing a historic fourth straight Asian Games title. Here's where the medal race and the football bracket stand.

## September 24 golds: shooting and fencing

- **Choo Ka-eun (shooting, women's 10m air pistol)**: Won gold with an Asian Games record score of 246.3 in the final, Korea's first title in the event since Kim Yun-mi at the 2010 Guangzhou Games — a 16-year gap.
- **Oh Sang-uk (fencing, men's individual sabre)**: Beat China's Sun Chenfeng 15-13 in the final to defend the title he won at Hangzhou 2022, extending Korea's run to five straight Asiad golds in the event.

## Football: Korea chases a fourth straight gold

Korea's men's U-23 team beat Saudi Arabia 2-0 in its opening group match and clinched a quarterfinal spot after just one game. That quarterfinal is today, September 25, against Vietnam, kicking off at 7:30 p.m. Korea time at Nagoya Mizuho Park Rugby Stadium. Korea has won Asian Games men's football gold three times running — Incheon 2014, Jakarta-Palembang 2018 and Hangzhou 2022 — and a win in Nagoya would make it the first team in the tournament's history to take four straight titles.

## Korea's target: 40-plus golds, third overall

Team Korea's stated goal for these Games is 40 or more gold medals and a third-place finish in the medal table. The 10 golds won by September 24 are a quarter of that target, but several of Korea's traditionally strong events — badminton (finals through September 29) and archery (September 26 through October 3) — are still ahead, so the gold count is expected to climb through the Games' back half.
