The 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games run through October 4, and the closing days are stacked: esports wraps up October 1–2, the women's football final falls on October 2, the men's football final on October 3, and the closing ceremony follows on October 4 at 18:00 at Nagoya's Mizuho Park Athletics Stadium. The headline event is that men's football final — Korea vs. Japan for the third Games running, after the same matchup decided gold in 2018 and 2022. South Korea's baseball team has already secured its own gold, beating Japan 3-1 on September 27 for a fifth consecutive Asian Games title. Here's the full closing-weekend schedule, venue by venue.

Most other sports — swimming, badminton, archery — finished their competitions earlier in the Games. Football, volleyball and esports are the last medal events still on the board before the closing ceremony.

## Men's Football Final: Korea vs. Japan, for the Third Games Running

Korea reached the final by beating China 2-1 in the semifinal, coming back from an early deficit. Japan needed extra time and penalties to get past Uzbekistan. The result sets up a Korea-Japan men's football final for the third consecutive Asian Games, after Japan won gold in 2018 (Jakarta-Palembang) and Korea won it in 2022 (Hangzhou). Kickoff is 19:30 on October 3 at Toyota Stadium in Aichi Prefecture.

## Women's Football Closes With Two Matches in One Day

The women's tournament wraps up entirely on October 2, with both medal matches at Shizuoka Stadium Ecopa, roughly 130 km from Nagoya. Korea plays China for bronze at 15:00, and Japan faces North Korea in the gold-medal match at 19:30. Korea's run ended in the semifinal with a loss to Japan.

## Baseball Gold Was Already Decided

Baseball finished before this final weekend, but it's worth knowing the result if you're catching up: Korea beat Japan 3-1 on September 27 for the gold medal, avenging an earlier 0-5 loss to the same opponent in the Super Round two days before. It's Korea's fifth consecutive Asian Games baseball gold, a streak that started at Guangzhou 2010 and has continued through Incheon 2014, Jakarta-Palembang 2018 and Hangzhou 2022.

## Esports Makes Its Full Medal Debut

This is the first Asian Games where League of Legends counts as a full medal sport rather than a demonstration event. Korea faces Saudi Arabia in the semifinal on October 1 at 13:30, with the gold-medal match on October 2 at noon, both at the Aichi Sky Expo. If the Korean lineup here has you interested in more competitive League of Legends, the four LCK teams that qualified for [LoL Worlds 2026](/en/posts/lol-worlds-2026-lck-teams-schedule/) start play on October 15.

## No Time Difference Between Korea and Japan

One practical note for anyone tracking these times from Korea: Japan and South Korea share the same time zone, so every kickoff and start time listed above is identical whether you're watching from Seoul or Nagoya. There's no conversion needed.

## Closing Ceremony: Date, Time and Venue

The closing ceremony runs from 18:00 to 19:30 on October 4 at Nagoya City Mizuho Park Athletics Stadium — the same 35,000-seat venue that hosted the opening ceremony on September 19.

## Planning a Last-Minute Trip for the Final Weekend

With three days left and marquee matches on the schedule, hotel availability in Nagoya and nearby Toyota and Shizuoka is tight through October 4. If you're weighing a short trip to catch the football final or closing ceremony in person, prioritize a room with free cancellation given how close the dates are — several finals venues (Toyota Stadium, Shizuoka Stadium Ecopa) sit outside central Nagoya, so factor in travel time when booking.
